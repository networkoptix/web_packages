Logging in to the CMS

Click on the Log in button and enter your email and password to log in to your company's CMS Portal.

•Check the Remember me checkbox to be automatically logged in when you open the site page.

•If necessary, you can use the Forgot Password button to generate an email that will allow you to reset your password.

 

The CMS portal opens to the Cloud Portal landing page. (To return to this page from the portal management site, click on View Site in the Cloud Administration header.)

Once you are logged on, the email address you logged in with will be displayed in the upper-right corner. Click on the arrow to the right of your email address to open the CMS account menu:

Account Settings

Opens a page where you can enter or edit your Email address, First name and Last name, and select a display Language. (To change the available languages contact Network Optix support; these cannot be updated from the CMS portal.)

Change Password

Opens a page where you can enter a new password by entering your current password.

Log Out

Click to log out of CMS and return to the Cloud portal landing page.

Administration

Click on this link to open the CMS dashboard. The available applications, and options within those, depend on your access privileges.

 

Cloud Portal Page Footer

Each Cloud Portal page has a set of footers that provide shortcuts to the following Cloud Portal pages:

•About {{Cloud Name}} – opens the CMS "Landing" page

•Download {{Client Name}} – opens your client download page

•Integrations – opens your integrations page

•Support – opens your CMS "Support" page

•Terms – opens your "License Agreement" page

•Privacy – opens your "Privacy Policy" pages

•Supported Devices – opens your Supported Devices page

 

Language menu

If you have requested that your customization support multiple languages, the footer will include a menu that lets users choose the display language for their Cloud Portal session.

Refer to "Selecting a language" for additional information concerning language display.
